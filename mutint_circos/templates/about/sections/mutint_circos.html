mutint-circos draws the reference genome as a circle with the mutations of the chosen sample marked around it (Circos, in the sidebar's experiment section, and a panel on the Overview). Each mark is coloured by its type; a deletion, amplification, inversion or conversion longer than 5 kb is drawn over its extent. A population can be drawn as one ring per time point, innermost earliest, to follow its mutations through time, and any number of samples as stacked tracks along a linear axis, ordered by treatment, population and time point and shaded the same way. The design follows genome_visualisation, which renders breseq output with NG-Circos; the drawing here is the plugin's own.
